Hush privacy · 10 November 2026

Your mic.
Your business.

Hush needs no account. The app contains no analytics, ads, or network service.

Mute without listening.

Mute controls, shortcuts, and capture-app detection do not read microphone audio. Basic controls need no microphone or Accessibility permission.

Levels are optional.

Live level monitoring requests mic access when you enable it. Hush processes audio on your Mac and saves no monitoring audio.

Tests stay with you.

The optional microphone test saves recordings and reports in ~/Library/Application Support/Hush/Tests/. You can remove those files in Finder.

Local history. A clear way to clear it.

Recent app names and use times stay in ~/Library/Application Support/Hush/capture-history.json. Hush keeps up to 100 records for 30 days. Clear history removes saved entries. Active apps can appear again while they continue to use the mic.

Settings and recovery.

Hush saves settings in macOS preferences. It saves input levels locally so it can restore them after a crash.

About this website.

This showcase contains illustrative mockups. Its interactive preview never requests mic access and does not control your Mac. The page adds no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting service can process routine connection and access data.

Other apps that use your microphone have their own privacy policies. Hush does not control how they process audio.
